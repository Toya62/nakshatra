## Phase 2: Specialized Diagnostic Report Catalog

Targeted, high-conversion diagnostic modules and exportable PDF/JSON reports:

### 1. 🪷 Full Life Kundali Report
- Comprehensive life reading synthesizing:
  - Core personality, physical vitality, and temperament (Lagna & Lagnesha).
  - Career path, status, and zenith (10th house, Amatyakaraka, D10 Dasamsha).
  - Wealth accumulation, family assets, and income streams (2nd and 11th houses).
  - Relationship profile, spouse characteristics, and domestic harmony (7th house, D9 Navamsha).
  - 10-year macro forecast based on operating Vimshottari Mahadashas.

### 2. ⏳ Vimshottari Dasha Life-Phases Guide
- Deep-dive into active and upcoming planetary periods:
  - Micro-period breakdown down to Pratyantardasha (PD) timestamps.
  - Psychological state, health challenges, and opportunities per sub-period.
  - "When will things change?" transit alignment trigger calculator.

### 3. 💕 Love, Marriage & Compatibility Guide
- Relationship potential and partner timing:
  - 7th house lord placement, Venus/Jupiter dignity, and Darakaraka analysis.
  - Marriage timing window scanner (Dasha activation of 2nd/7th/11th lords + Jupiter double transit).
  - Automated 36-point Ashtakoota compatibility scoring with Kuja Dosha cross-matching.

### 4. 🪐 Saturn & Sade Sati Diagnostic Report
- Dedicated 7.5-year Saturn transit impact assessment:
  - Current phase: Rising (12th from Moon), Peak (Moon sign), or Setting (2nd from Moon).
  - Small Panoti checks: Kantaka Shani (4th from Moon) and Ashtama Shani (8th from Moon).
  - Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) bindu mitigation analysis.
  - Classical remedies: Saturday Chhaya Dāna, mustard oil lamp, Shani Beej Mantra, black dog seva.

### 5. ♂️ Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha) Analyzer
- Mars affliction evaluation:
  - Mars in 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th from Lagna, Moon, and Venus.
  - High vs. Low Manglik severity classification.
  - Strict classical cancellation checklist (own signs Aries/Scorpio, exalted Capricorn, Kendra Jupiter aspect, partner chart matching).
  - Remedial rituals and Kumbh Vivah guidance.

### 6. 🐍 Kalsarp Dosha Analyzer
- Rahu–Ketu nodal axis diagnostic:
  - Detection of all 7 classical planets hemmed between Rahu and Ketu.
  - Identification of 12 classical Kalsarp types:
    1. *Anant* (1–7 axis)
    2. *Kulik* (2–8 axis)
    3. *Vasuki* (3–9 axis)
    4. *Shankhpal* (4–10 axis)
    5. *Padma* (5–11 axis)
    6. *Mahapadma* (6–12 axis)
    7. *Takshak* (7–1 axis)
    8. *Karkotak* (8–2 axis)
    9. *Shankhachur* (9–3 axis)
    10. *Ghatak* (10–4 axis)
    11. *Vishdhar* (11–5 axis)
    12. *Sheshnag* (12–6 axis)
  - Partial (Ardh) vs. Complete Kalsarp classification with Rahu Shanti upayas.

### 7. 🪔 Pitra Dosha (Ancestral Karma) Report
- Karmic debt and generational imprint analyzer:
  - Sun afflicted by Rahu/Saturn in 9th house, 9th lord in 8th/12th, or debilitated Jupiter.
  - Psychological legacy patterns (delayed lineage prosperity, unexplainable friction).
  - Dharma-aligned remedies: Amavasya tarpana, feeding cows and crows, Peepal tree jal-arpana.

### 8. 👑 Raj Yoga & Status Index
- Power and prosperity combinations:
  - Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10 Vishnu sthanas) $\times$ Trikona (1, 5, 9 Lakshmi sthanas) lord conjunctions and mutual aspects.
  - Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (Ruchaka, Bhadra, Hamsa, Malavya, Sasa).
  - Parivartana Maha Yogas and Gaja Kesari strength audit.

### 9. 💎 Gemstone & Metal Prescription Engine
- Precision Vedic Ratna therapy:
  - Favorable functional benefic identification (avoiding stones for functional malefics/Dusthana lords).
  - Gemstone weight calculation (carats/ratti based on body mass and planet strength).
  - Metal recommendation (Gold, Silver, Panchadhatu, Copper).
  - Ideal wearing finger, day, and auspicious Hora muhurtha.
  - Strict cautions and substitute (Upratna) alternatives.

### 10. ✈️ Videsh Yoga (Foreign Travel & Relocation)
- Global settlement and foreign opportunity scanner:
  - 9th house (long journeys, higher wisdom), 12th house (foreign residence, overseas expenditure), and 3rd house (short travel) axis.
  - Rahu (karaka for foreign lands) and Moon (water/travel karaka) placements.
  - Timing foreign relocation via Dasha activations of 9th/12th lords.

## Phase 3: Astrology Academy & Knowledge Hub

Educational content pillars designed for organic discovery (SEO) and user onboarding:

### Vedic Astrology Foundations
- **12 Zodiac Signs (Rashis)**:
  - Mesha (Aries) through Meena (Pisces): elemental trines (Fire, Earth, Air, Water), gunas (Sattva, Rajas, Tamas), ruling Grahas, and symbol significations.
- **9 Classical Grahas**:
  - Sun (Atma), Moon (Manas), Mars (Energy), Mercury (Buddhi), Jupiter (Guru/Dharma), Venus (Sukra/Shukra), Saturn (Karma/Shani), Rahu (Desire), Ketu (Moksha).
- **12 Bhavas (Houses)**:
  - Tanu, Dhana, Sahaja, Sukha, Putra, Ari, Yuvati, Randhra, Dharma, Karma, Labha, Vyaya.
- **27 Nakshatras**:
  - Ashwini to Revati: 4 padas, ruling deities, planetary lords, and Nakshatra temperaments (Deva, Manushya, Rakshasa).

### KP Astrology (Krishnamurti Paddhati)
- Sub-lord theory: division of each Nakshatra into 9 proportional sub-lords.
- Placidus cusp house division with sidereal zodiac.
- Ruling Planets (RP) technique for instant horary (Prashna) queries.
- Sharper event verification without ambiguous generalities.

### Vedic Numerology (Ank Jyotish)
- Meaning of Numbers 1 through 9:
  - Number 1 (Sun), 2 (Moon), 3 (Jupiter), 4 (Rahu), 5 (Mercury), 6 (Venus), 7 (Ketu), 8 (Saturn), 9 (Mars).
- Core calculations:
  - **Mulank (Birth Number)**: Core personality derived from day of birth.
  - **Bhagyank (Destiny/Life Path Number)**: Life trajectory derived from full date sum.
  - Name numerology (Chaldean / Cheiro method) for brand and personal name harmonization.

### Western Astrology System
- Core Sun-Sign system comparison:
  - Tropical vs. Sidereal zodiac explanation.
  - The 12 Western Sun signs & Ascendant/Rising sign fundamentals.
  - Outer Planets: Uranus (Innovation/Revolution), Neptune (Illusion/Mysticism), Pluto (Transformation/Power).
  - Major Western Aspects: Conjunction ($0^\circ$), Sextile ($60^\circ$), Square ($90^\circ$), Trine ($120^\circ$), Opposition ($180^\circ$).
