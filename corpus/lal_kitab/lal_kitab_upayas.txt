Chapter 1, Verse 1: General Principles and Divine Rules of Lal Kitab Upayas (Remedies). All Lal Kitab remedies must strictly be performed in the daytime between sunrise and sunset; remedies performed during the night are strictly prohibited and can cause reverse malefic effects. Only one remedy should be performed at a time for an afflicted planet, consistently maintained for forty to forty-three consecutive days without interruption. If any day is missed in the sequence, the entire counting must be restarted. A remedy must never be performed for a planet sitting in its own house or in an exalted sign, as doing so will destroy its auspicious strength.

Chapter 1, Verse 2: Inviolable Precautions (Savdhanis) of Lal Kitab. Every upaya carries an accompanying behavioral boundary. Accepting free gifts, charity, or alms without payment severely damages Saturn and Jupiter, activating malefic karmic debts. Keeping unserviceable electric goods, rusted clocks, or broken metal items on the roof or inside the dwelling severely aggravates Rahu and causes unexpected litigation or financial losses. Destroying sacred trees, keeping broad-leaved green indoor plants, or possessing birds in cages severely impairs Mercury and ruins commercial intellect.

Chapter 1, Verse 3: The Concept of Soe Hue Ghar (Sleeping Houses) and Kismat Jagana. When a house has no planetary occupant and receives no direct aspect, it is termed a sleeping house (soya hua ghar). To awaken a sleeping house of fortune, the native must perform the symbolic activation of its natural karaka. To activate the 1st house (Self), offer water to Sun or donate jaggery; to awaken the 2nd house (Wealth), visit places of worship and honor elders; to awaken the 9th house (Destiny/Bhagya), perform selfless seva in temples or place saffron tilak on the forehead.

Chapter 2, Verse 1: Sun (Surya) in 1st House (Lagna) Upayas and Precautions. When Sun is afflicted in the 1st house, causing excessive ego, eye troubles, or disputes with father and state authorities, the native must construct a water handpump or tap for public welfare. The native must refrain from installing an oven or cooking hearth in the front veranda or entrance of the house. Consuming a small morsel of jaggery with water prior to stepping out for important endeavors ensures success and pacifies solar affliction.

Chapter 2, Verse 2: Sun (Surya) in 2nd House Upayas and Precautions. For afflicted Sun in the 2nd house causing family disharmony or dental and financial distress, the native should donate coconut, mustard oil, or whole badam (almonds) at religious shrines. Precaution: Never accept gifts of gold, brass, or yellow cloth from in-laws or maternal relatives. Maintaining a silver coin gifted by the mother wrapped in yellow silk safeguards the family wealth and eliminates domestic friction.

Chapter 2, Verse 3: Sun (Surya) in 6th and 7th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Sun in the 7th house creates severe discord with the spouse and financial dryness in partnerships. The supreme Lal Kitab upaya is to bury a piece of square bronze or copper plate under the earth in an uninhabited, desolate place. Refrain from conducting marriage ceremonies at night or during twilight. For Sun in the 6th house, feeding jaggery and roasted wheat flour to red-faced monkeys or cows neutralizes ancestral debts and dispels chronic health ailments.

Chapter 2, Verse 4: Sun (Surya) in 8th and 10th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Sun in the 8th house brings sudden government penalties and cardiac stress. The native must immerse copper coins or round copper plates in flowing river water (Jal Pravah) on Sundays. Never live in a south-facing main entrance house. For Sun in the 10th house, never wear blue or black garments; wearing a white cap or keeping the head covered during public work enhances administrative authority and protects career reputation.

Chapter 3, Verse 1: Moon (Chandra) in 1st and 2nd Houses Upayas and Precautions. Moon rules peace of mind (Manas), mother, and liquid wealth. When Moon is debilitated or aspected by Rahu/Saturn in 1st or 2nd houses causing depression, emotional turbulence, and loss of ancestral inheritance, the native must obtain a solid silver square plate (chandi ka thos tukda) from the mother with her blessing and preserve it permanently in a safe. The native must never sell silver ancestral jewelry or purchase land from childless relatives.

Chapter 3, Verse 2: Moon (Chandra) in 6th, 7th, and 8th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Moon in the 6th house is highly vulnerable to psychic disturbances and respiratory congestion. Precaution: The native must never drink plain milk during the night time, and must never provide free water or dig a public well without paying wages. For Moon in the 7th house, the native must not marry before age twenty-four without parental consent, and should donate milk mixed with sugar to girls. For Moon in the 8th house, offering water to guests and maintaining silver vessels at home wards off mental agony.

Chapter 3, Verse 3: Moon (Chandra) in 10th, 11th, and 12th Houses Upayas and Precautions. When Moon occupies the 12th house (the house of isolation and drainage), the native must never provide free educational books or stationary to strangers, as it drains personal luck. The native must regularly visit sacred rivers and immerse raw rice washed in milk into flowing water. For Moon in the 10th house, serving milk to elderly pilgrims and abstaining from alcohol guarantees unyielding professional advancement.

Chapter 4, Verse 1: Mars (Mangal) Shubh vs Mangal Baddh Principles. In Lal Kitab, Mars is divided into Mangal Nek (benevolent, righteous protector) and Mangal Baddh (poisonous, afflicted Mars causing accident, blood disorders, and destructive wrath). When Mars is placed with Saturn, Sun, or aspected by Ketu, it becomes Mangal Baddh. The paramount remedy for Mangal Baddh is to bury sweet honey in an earthen pot (mitti ka bartan) in a desolate graveyard or barren land, or float sweet batashas (sugar candies) in flowing water on Tuesdays.

Chapter 4, Verse 2: Mars (Mangal) in 1st, 4th, 7th, and 8th Houses (Lal Kitab Kuja Dosha). For severe Mangal affliction in the marital houses causing discord, domestic violence, or widowhood threats: Mars in 1st requires feeding sweet rotis (baked bread sweetened with jaggery) to street dogs. Mars in 4th demands wearing pure silver around the neck and avoiding broad wooden roofs. Mars in 7th requires immersing round copper discs in running water and serving maternal uncles. Mars in 8th requires baking eight sweet rotis and distributing them to street animals.

Chapter 4, Verse 3: Mars (Mangal) in 3rd, 6th, and 10th Houses Upayas. For Mars in the 3rd house, keep ivory or deer horn at home, and wear silver rings without joints. For Mars in the 6th house, distributing salty foods on the birth of a male child instead of sweets pacifies malefic enemies. For Mars in the 10th house (the exalted throne of Mars), the native must always maintain a gold ring or copper vessel at home, speak truth, and never sell ancestral gold during hardships.

Chapter 5, Verse 1: Mercury (Budh) in 1st, 3rd, and 6th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Mercury governs business intellect, speech, nervous system, and trade. When afflicted by Rahu or Ketu, it creates stammering, cognitive fog, and debt. For Mercury in the 1st house, avoid green clothes, and donate green moong dal wrapped in brass vessels to temples. For Mercury in the 3rd house, keep a clean caged parrot or bird prohibited; instead, feed wet green grass to cows. For Mercury in the 6th house, wear a solid silver ring on the little finger and avoid gambling.

Chapter 5, Verse 2: Mercury (Budh) in 8th, 9th, and 12th Houses (Budh Baddh Upayas). Mercury in the 8th or 12th house is considered highly tricky, generating sudden bankruptcies and intellectual paralysis. Remedy: Pierce the nose (nak chhedan) and wear a silver ring in the nose for ninety-six days, or float an unbaked earthen pot filled with whole green moong beans into a flowing river on Wednesdays. Precaution: Never keep broad-leaved indoor plants, money plants, or broken musical instruments inside the living quarters.

Chapter 6, Verse 1: Jupiter (Guru) in 1st, 2nd, and 5th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Jupiter represents divine grace, wisdom, progeny, and spiritual preceptors. When afflicted by Rahu (Guru Chandal Yoga) or placed in inimical signs, it leads to liver disease, loss of honor, and financial stagnation. For Jupiter in 1st house, always apply saffron (kesar) or turmeric tilak on the forehead and navel every morning. For Jupiter in 2nd house, donate yellow gram dal (chana dal) and turmeric roots wrapped in yellow cloth to holy shrines on Thursdays.

Chapter 6, Verse 2: Jupiter (Guru) in 6th, 7th, 8th, and 10th Houses Upayas. Jupiter in the 6th or 10th house demands absolute humility: never donate clothes to religious ascetics or sanyasis, and never offer free food at religious places if Jupiter is afflicted in 10th. For Jupiter in 7th house, donate yellow lemons and gold foil in temple. For Jupiter in 8th house, wear gold continuously around the neck and plant a peepal tree in an open public park (not inside residential boundaries).

Chapter 7, Verse 1: Venus (Shukra) in 1st, 2nd, and 6th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Venus rules marital bliss, physical comfort, beauty, and vehicles. When Venus is debilitated in Virgo (6th house), it brings marital alienation, diabetes, and poverty. The classical Lal Kitab remedy for Venus in the 6th house is to donate a milch cow (or feed dough balls made of wheat flour with butter to a white cow) every Friday. The spouse should wear gold hairpins or ornaments to protect marital longevity.

Chapter 7, Verse 2: Venus (Shukra) in 7th, 8th, and 12th Houses Upayas and Precautions. For Venus in the 7th house, feeding white domesticated cows and maintaining clean, pleasant fragrances at home repels misfortune. For Venus in the 8th house, throw blue flowers in dirty gutters or floating canals for forty-three days, and never accept free bronze or perfumes. For Venus in the 12th house (where Venus is traditionally exalted), the native should keep a square piece of silver under the pillow and respect women unfailingly.

Chapter 8, Verse 1: Saturn (Shani) in 1st, 2nd, and 4th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Saturn is the cosmic judge (Dharmadhikari). When Saturn sits in 1st house, the native must never tell lies or consume alcohol; pouring oil on earth or offering sweet mustard oil bread to black dogs dispels delays. For Saturn in 2nd house, serving religious temples on foot and maintaining barefoot pilgrimage on Saturdays brings tremendous stability. For Saturn in 4th house, never drink milk from black cows, and pour milk into a well.

Chapter 8, Verse 2: Saturn (Shani) in 7th, 8th, and 10th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Saturn in 7th house requires burying an earthen pot filled with pure mustard oil in uncultivated soil, or donating an iron griddle (tawa) or tongs (chimta) to poor cooks on Saturdays. For Saturn in 8th house, throw eight whole almonds into running water and bring half back home. For Saturn in 10th house, never accept free alcoholic beverages, and feed ten visually impaired individuals or daily wage laborers.

Chapter 8, Verse 3: Saturn (Shani) Sade Sati Special Lal Kitab Upayas. During the seven-and-a-half-year Sade Sati or during Ashtama Shani: (1) Fill an iron bowl with mustard oil, look at one's own reflection in the oil (Chhaya Dāna), and donate it along with coins to a Dakaut or laborer on Saturday. (2) Keep a black dog at home or feed rotis smeared with mustard oil to stray black dogs. (3) Wear a pure iron ring crafted from the horseshoe of a black horse or nail from a boat on the middle finger of the right hand.

Chapter 9, Verse 1: Rahu in 1st, 5th, and 8th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Rahu symbolizes illusion, sudden catastrophe, smoke, and electronic turmoil. When Rahu afflicts the Lagna or 5th house causing phobias, miscarriages, or sudden financial collapse, the native must immerse barley (jau) washed in cow's milk into flowing water. Keep a small silver elephant with solid silver legs in the north-east corner of the living room. Precaution: Never keep useless electrical scrap, broken glass, or rusted iron items on the rooftop.

Chapter 9, Verse 2: Rahu in 7th, 9th, and 12th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Rahu in the 7th house creates deceptive marital relationships and partnership betrayals. Remedy: Fill an earthen pot with river water and silver coins, seal it tightly, and bury it in deep soil. For Rahu in the 12th house (the bedroom/drainage house), the native must eat meals while sitting inside the kitchen area on a wooden mat (chowki), never in the bedroom. Keep saunf (fennel seeds) in a red pouch under the pillow to prevent nightmares.

Chapter 9, Verse 3: Ketu in 1st, 6th, and 12th Houses Upayas and Precautions. Ketu is the flag of karma, renunciation, joint pains, and urinary ailments. For Ketu in 1st or 6th house, wear a solid gold ring in the left hand, and feed two-colored (black-and-white / kabra) dogs with bread smeared in butter. For Ketu in the 12th house, donate sesame seeds (til) and a dual-colored blanket (kambal) to a temple or homeless ascetic on Wednesday mornings.

Chapter 10, Verse 1: Lal Kitab Pitri Rina (Ancestral Debt) Identification and Upayas. Pitri Rina is indicated when Jupiter is afflicted by Saturn, Rahu, or Mercury in Kendra houses, causing chronic illness across generations and lack of family lineage continuation. Classical Upaya: Collect an equal amount of money or yellow grams from every living blood relative (father, uncles, brothers, sisters, children) and with that joint sum perform a collective feast or donation of food and holy scripture at a revered ancient shrine.

Chapter 10, Verse 2: Matri Rina (Mother's Debt) and Stri Rina (Spouse's Debt) Upayas. When Moon or Venus are severely afflicted in the 2nd, 7th, or 8th houses accompanied by Ketu or Mars, Matri Rina or Stri Rina manifests as mental agony, infertility, or sudden family ruin. For Matri Rina, collect silver coins from all blood relatives and immerse them collectively into a sacred holy river. For Stri Rina, feed one hundred cows with fresh green grass or donate white cows to deserving sanctuaries.
