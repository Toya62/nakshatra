Chapter 1, Verse 1: General Principles and Divine Rules of Lal Kitab Upayas (Remedies). All Lal Kitab remedies must strictly be performed in the daytime between sunrise and sunset; remedies performed during the night are strictly prohibited and can cause reverse malefic effects. Only one remedy should be performed at a time for an afflicted planet, consistently maintained for forty to forty-three consecutive days without interruption. If any day is missed in the sequence, the entire counting must be restarted. A remedy must never be performed for a planet sitting in its own house or in an exalted sign, as doing so will destroy its auspicious strength.

Chapter 1, Verse 2: Inviolable Precautions (Savdhanis) of Lal Kitab. Every upaya carries an accompanying behavioral boundary. Accepting free gifts, charity, or alms without payment severely damages Saturn and Jupiter, activating malefic karmic debts. Keeping unserviceable electric goods, rusted clocks, or broken metal items on the roof or inside the dwelling severely aggravates Rahu and causes unexpected litigation or financial losses. Destroying sacred trees, keeping broad-leaved green indoor plants, or possessing birds in cages severely impairs Mercury and ruins commercial intellect.

Chapter 1, Verse 3: The Concept of Soe Hue Ghar (Sleeping Houses) and Kismat Jagana. When a house has no planetary occupant and receives no direct aspect, it is termed a sleeping house (soya hua ghar). To awaken a sleeping house of fortune, the native must perform the symbolic activation of its natural karaka. To activate the 1st house (Self), offer water to Sun or donate jaggery; to awaken the 2nd house (Wealth), visit places of worship and honor elders; to awaken the 9th house (Destiny/Bhagya), perform selfless seva in temples or place saffron tilak on the forehead.
