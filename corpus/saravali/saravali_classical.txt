Chapter 21, Verse 1: Sun in Aries (Mesha) and Taurus (Vrishabha). Sun exalted in Aries (Mesha) makes the native renowned, courageous, energetic, skilled in statecraft or warfare, commanding authoritative leadership, and devoted to righteous deeds. Sun in Taurus (Vrishabha) makes the native skilled in trade, fond of perfumes, arts, textiles, and water sports, but stubborn in opinion and susceptible to oral or facial fatigue.

Chapter 21, Verse 2: Sun in Gemini (Mithuna) and Cancer (Karka). Sun in Gemini makes the native learned, scholarly, wealthy, proficient in grammar, astrology, literature, and diplomatic discourse, with a pleasant and adaptable demeanor. Sun in Cancer makes the native emotionally sensitive, fond of ancestral homesteads, deeply attached to family, enduring in physical labor, and given to changes of residence.

Chapter 21, Verse 3: Sun in Leo (Simha) and Virgo (Kanya). Sun in own sign Leo makes the native majestic, victorious over rivals, high-minded, energetic, a natural ruler of men, generous to dependents, and uncompromising in self-respect. Sun in Virgo makes the native analytical, skilled in writing, mathematics, crafts, and medical sciences, soft-spoken, and possessing a modest, disciplined constitution.

Chapter 21, Verse 4: Sun in Libra (Tula) and Scorpio (Vrischika). Sun debilitated in Libra makes the native face initial struggles with self-confidence, authoritative superiors, and balance of partnerships, requiring conscious cultivation of self-worth and legal prudence. Sun in Scorpio makes the native secretive, bold, formidable in research or military strategy, persistent in challenges, and possessing fiery willpower.

Chapter 21, Verse 5: Sun in Sagittarius (Dhanus) and Capricorn (Makara). Sun in Sagittarius makes the native pious, revered by scholars and preceptors, rich in moral wisdom, philosophical, a counselor to leaders, and honorable in public life. Sun in Capricorn makes the native industrious, persevering through early obstacles, prudent in financial savings, quiet, and achieving steady success through disciplined craftsmanship.

Chapter 21, Verse 6: Sun in Aquarius (Kumbha) and Pisces (Meena). Sun in Aquarius makes the native engaged in collective social movements, philosophical, detached from superficial vanities, and persevering in long-term goals. Sun in Pisces makes the native compassionate, fond of holy shrines, blessed with pearl and marine trade, prosperous through maternal or spiritual endowments, and possessing peaceful speech.

Chapter 22, Verse 1: Moon in Aries (Mesha) and Taurus (Vrishabha). Moon in Aries gives swift movement, pioneering enthusiasm, round eyes, courageous temperament, and independence of spirit. Moon exalted in Taurus gives extraordinary physical grace, sweet voice, love of luxury and fine foods, unwavering emotional stability, enduring wealth, and affectionate fidelity.

Chapter 22, Verse 2: Moon in Gemini (Mithuna) and Cancer (Karka). Moon in Gemini produces poetic wit, eloquence, skill in commerce and languages, charming humor, and intellectual curiosity. Moon in own sign Cancer gives tender maternal affection, deep intuition, agricultural and real estate wealth, popularity among friends, and emotional responsiveness.

Chapter 22, Verse 3: Moon in Leo (Simha) and Virgo (Kanya). Moon in Leo gives noble carriage, magnanimous heart, dislike of subordination, courageous pride, and fondness for open nature and honors. Moon in Virgo gives modesty, analytical mind, mastery of music, arts, and scriptures, gentle manners, and financial precision.

Chapter 22, Verse 4: Moon in Libra (Tula) and Scorpio (Vrischika). Moon in Libra gives refined aesthetic taste, graceful partnerships, diplomacy, and love of fair justice and trade. Moon debilitated in Scorpio gives intense, brooding emotions, deep occult insight, secretive determination, and transformational emotional depth requiring calm mindfulness.

Chapter 22, Verse 5: Moon in Sagittarius (Dhanus) and Capricorn (Makara). Moon in Sagittarius gives religious devotion, upright moral character, generous hospitality, mastery of philosophy, and broad vision. Moon in Capricorn gives cautious practicality, organizational endurance, emotional resilience under austerity, and steady climb to authority.

Chapter 22, Verse 6: Moon in Aquarius (Kumbha) and Pisces (Meena). Moon in Aquarius gives humanitarian idealism, intuitive grasp of social psychology, independence, and meditative vision. Moon in Pisces gives profound spiritual intuition, love of sacred waters, artistic genius, compassionate heart, and peaceful disposition.

Chapter 31, Verse 1: Conjunction of Sun and Mercury (Budhaditya Yoga). When the Sun and Mercury are conjoined in a single house, Budhaditya Yoga is formed. The native becomes highly intelligent, eloquent, renowned for scholarship, skilled in administrative advisory roles, respected by sovereigns, and blessed with enduring learning and mathematical acumen.

Chapter 31, Verse 2: Conjunction of Sun and Jupiter (Surya-Guru Yoga). When the Sun and Jupiter are conjoined, the native is deeply righteous, devoted to spiritual teachers, endowed with lofty moral stature, a trusted advisor to ministers or kings, wealthy, truthful, and blessed with spotless reputation.

Chapter 31, Verse 3: Conjunction of Moon and Mars (Chandra-Mangala Yoga). When the Moon and Mars are conjoined in a house, Chandra-Mangala Yoga is generated. The native earns abundant wealth through enterprise, metallurgy, real estate, and trade. The native possesses intense passion, practical courage, and strong business drive.

Chapter 31, Verse 4: Conjunction of Moon and Jupiter (Gaja Kesari Conjunction). When the Moon and Jupiter unite in one house, the person attains radiant fame, profound ethical judgment, immense wealth, universal affection, and noble leadership in education, state, or spiritual institutions.

Chapter 31, Verse 5: Conjunction of Mars and Venus (Mangal-Shukra Yoga). When Mars and Venus are conjoined, the native is passionately artistic, attractive to the opposite sex, fond of luxurious ornament, passionate in romance, skilled in performance, and requiring mindfulness in marital commitments.

Chapter 31, Verse 6: Conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn (Guru-Shani Yoga). When Jupiter and Saturn unite, the native possesses deep philosophical maturity, institutional vision, enduring patience, mastery of law and ancient traditions, and authority over large organizations and public works.

Chapter 34, Verse 1: Foundations of Raja Yogas in Saravali. Raja Yogas (combinations for executive power and sovereignty) arise primarily when the lords of the Kendra houses (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) form mutual relationships, conjunctions, or aspects with the lords of the Trikona houses (1st, 5th, 9th). The union of the 9th lord (fortune/dharma) and the 10th lord (action/karma) forms the supreme Dharma-Karmadhipati Raja Yoga, elevating the native to highest authority and noble fame.

Chapter 34, Verse 2: Raja Yogas Formed by Benefics in Kendras. When all Kendra houses are occupied solely by natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, and waxing Moon) without malefic association, the native is elevated to the rank of a ruler or leader of men, blessed with longevity, prosperity, and unblemished honor.

Chapter 35, Verse 1: Nabhasa Yogas: Ashraya and Dala Yogas. Nabhasa Yogas operate throughout life without being confined to particular dasha periods. The Ashraya Yogas depend on the signs occupied: Rajju (planets in movable signs) gives fondness for travel and ambition; Musala (planets in fixed signs) gives steadfast determination, immovable wealth, and unwavering purpose; Nala (planets in dual signs) gives adaptable ingenuity and intellectual flexibility.

Chapter 32, Verse 1: Kalatra Bhava and Characteristics of the Spouse. According to Kalyanavarma in Saravali, the nature, beauty, and temperament of the spouse are discerned from the sign falling on the 7th house, its ruling planet, and the planets aspecting it. If the 7th house is ruled by Venus or Jupiter, the spouse is graceful, virtuous, endowed with modesty, sweet-spoken, and born of an honorable family. If ruled by Mercury, the spouse is intelligent, witty, skilled in business, and youthful. If ruled by Mars or the Sun, the partner is bold, energetic, commanding, and possesses high self-respect. If ruled by Saturn, the partner is steadfast, quiet, serious, and deeply devoted to duties.

Chapter 32, Verse 2: Planetary Conjunctions Influencing Conjugal Happiness. When natural benefics cast their full aspects on the 7th house and Venus is situated in an auspicious Rashi and Navamsha, the marriage is blessed with lifelong joy, mutual respect, and prosperity. If malefic planets aspect the 7th house, the couple must cultivate conscious patience, communication, and emotional self-discipline to transcend differences in temperament and forge a resilient, harmonious bond.

Chapter 33, Verse 1: Karma Bhava and Professional Heights in Saravali. The 10th house is the zenith of worldly action. A strong 10th lord situated in Kendra or Trikona with benefic aspects elevates the native to prominence in their chosen profession. When the Sun, Mars, or Jupiter occupies the 10th house, the native attains executive responsibility, widespread recognition, and victory in public endeavors.

Chapter 33, Verse 2: Professional Synthesis from the Lagna and the Moon. To evaluate livelihood and occupation accurately, examine the 10th house counted both from the Lagna and from the Janma Rashi (Moon sign). The stronger of the two houses, along with its lord and Navamsha dispositor, reveals the native's true vocational aptitude, whether in leadership, statecraft, commerce, arts, sciences, or engineering.
