Chapter 1, Verse 1: The Seven Chara Karakas of Jaimini. In the Jaimini system of Jyotisha, seven variable planetary significators (Chara Karakas) are determined based on the descending order of their longitudinal degrees, minutes, and seconds within their respective signs (excluding Rahu and Ketu in the standard 7-karaka scheme): (1) Atmakaraka (planet with highest degree) representing the soul, core vitality, and life lesson; (2) Amatyakaraka (second highest degree) representing the career, ministerial council, and profession; (3) Bhratrukaraka (third highest) representing siblings, mentors, and spiritual guides; (4) Matrukaraka (fourth highest) representing mother, emotional foundation, and domestic shelter; (5) Putrakaraka (fifth highest) representing progeny, intellect, creativity, and disciples; (6) Gnatikaraka (sixth highest) representing adversaries, obstacles, illness, and competitive struggle; and (7) Darakaraka (seventh, lowest degree) representing the spouse, intimate partnerships, and trade relationships.

Chapter 1, Verse 2: Atmakaraka and Soul Destiny. The Atmakaraka (AK) is the king of the chart. It signifies the principal spiritual curriculum that the soul has reincarnated to master. Whichever sign or house the Atmakaraka occupies in the Rashi (D1) and Navamsha (D9) reveals the central arena of karmic evolution. When the Atmakaraka is an auspicious planet placed in strength, the native easily discovers dharmic direction; when placed in Dusthanas or under malefic aspect, the native undergoes profound inner refinement through patience and selfless surrender.

Chapter 1, Verse 3: Amatyakaraka and Career Achievement. The Amatyakaraka (AmK) governs livelihood, professional advisors, and vocational success. If the Amatyakaraka is strongly placed in the 1st, 5th, 9th, 10th, or 11th houses from the Lagna or from the Arudha Lagna, the native attains eminent professional rank, trusted advisory positions to governments or leaders, and lasting financial stability. If the Atmakaraka and Amatyakaraka form a mutual Kendra, Trikona, or conjunction, a supreme Jaimini Raja Yoga is created, guaranteeing high executive standing.

Chapter 1, Verse 4: Darakaraka and Relationship Dynamics. The Darakaraka (DK) represents the spouse and relational karma. The nature of the planet acting as Darakaraka describes the character, temperament, and appearance of the partner: Sun as DK indicates an authoritative, dignified partner; Moon as DK indicates an intuitive, emotional partner; Mars as DK indicates an energetic, courageous partner; Mercury as DK indicates an articulate, youthful partner; Jupiter as DK indicates a wise, virtuous, and spiritual partner; Venus as DK indicates an artistic, refined partner; and Saturn as DK indicates a mature, prudent, and persevering partner.

Chapter 1, Verse 5: Karakamsa Lagna and Spiritual Evolution. The sign in the Navamsha (D9) chart occupied by the Atmakaraka is termed the Karakamsa. Analyzing the Karakamsa Lagna and the 12 houses counted from it reveals the soul's deepest spiritual inclination and innate intellectual talents. Benefics in the 12th house from the Karakamsa (especially Ketu) signify spiritual liberation (Moksha) and higher contemplative states. The 1st and 5th houses from the Karakamsa reveal natural creative genius and philosophical orientation.

Chapter 1, Verse 6: Arudha Lagna (Pada Lagna) and Social Image. Arudha Lagna (AL) is calculated by counting how many houses the Lagna lord is removed from the Lagna, and projecting that same distance forward from the Lagna lord's position. The Arudha Lagna signifies the native's external reputation, social standing, material manifestation, and how the world perceives the native. Benefic planets (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) placed in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, or 11th houses from the Arudha Lagna create immense prosperity, spotless public honor, and material success.

Chapter 1, Verse 7: Upapada Lagna (UL) and Marital Longevity. Upapada Lagna (UL or Gaunapada) is the Arudha of the 12th house. It is the primary Jaimini indicator for marriage, the partner's family background, and the stability of marital life. If benefic planets occupy or aspect the Upapada Lagna and its 2nd house, the marriage is harmonious, long-lasting, and blessed with virtuous children. If the 2nd house from the Upapada is afflicted by malefic planets (Sun, Saturn, Rahu, Mars) or debilitated planets, it indicates friction in partnership requiring conscious patience, mutual respect, and dedicated Upayas.

Chapter 1, Verse 8: Argala and Virodhargala (Intervention and Obstruction). In the Jaimini system, planets exert Argala (intervention or decisive influence) on other houses. Planets in the 2nd, 4th, and 11th houses from any reference sign cause primary Argala upon that sign. Planets in the 5th house cause secondary Argala. However, this Argala is obstructed (Virodhargala) if planets occupy the corresponding obstruction houses: planets in the 12th obstruct the 2nd; planets in the 10th obstruct the 4th; planets in the 3rd obstruct the 11th; and planets in the 9th obstruct the 5th. Benefic Argala brings effortless assistance and fortune, while malefic Argala demands disciplined fortitude.

Chapter 2, Verse 1: Foundations of Jaimini Chara Dasha. Unlike Vimshottari Dasha which is based on Nakshatras and planetary periods, Jaimini Chara Dasha is a sign-based (Rashi) dasha system. The dasha periods are counted through the 12 zodiacal signs in regular (direct/Savya) or reverse (indirect/Apasavya) order depending on whether the starting sign is odd or even, and whether the 9th house from it is odd or even. The duration of each sign dasha (from 1 to 12 years) is determined by the distance of the sign's lord from that sign. Chara Dasha accurately pinpoints major life events, relocations, career elevations, and marital timing by examining the transit of Karakas through the operating Dasha signs.
