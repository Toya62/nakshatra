Chapter 2, Verse 1: Planetary Significations of Sun, Moon, and Mars. In Uttara Kalamrita, Mahakavi Kalidasa details the exhaustive karakattwas (significations). The Sun governs the soul (Atman), king, sovereign authority, father, copper, ruby, bone system, courage, vitality, places of worship, victory in battle, self-reliance, and administrative power. The Moon governs the mind (Manas), mother, pearl, liquid wealth, silver, agriculture, pleasant dwellings, sleep, travel across water, emotional sweetness, and pure white garments. Mars governs younger brothers, physical strength, land, copper, coral, military command, weapons, surgery, courage, competitive drive, fire, blood, and administrative energy.

Chapter 2, Verse 2: Planetary Significations of Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, and Saturn. Mercury governs intellect (Buddhi), maternal uncle, trade, speech, emerald, mathematics, literature, astrology, versatility, youth, nervous system, and diplomacy. Jupiter governs divine wisdom (Jnana), children (Putra), preceptors, guru, gold, yellow topaz, fat tissue, devotion to deities, righteousness, legal judgments, banking, and spiritual liberation. Venus governs spouse (Kalatra), vehicles, diamond, fine arts, poetry, scents, reproductive vitality, silver, artistic conveyances, beauty, and refined pleasures. Saturn governs longevity (Ayus), servants, iron, blue sapphire, endurance, patience, discipline, agriculture, oil, renunciation, public masses, and victory achieved through humble persistence.

Chapter 2, Verse 3: Planetary Significations of Rahu and Ketu. Rahu governs sudden fortunes, unconventional sciences, foreign languages, electronics, agate (gomedha), aviation, deceptive maneuvers, ancestral paternal lineage, and material ambition. Ketu governs spiritual enlightenment (Moksha), cat's eye gemstone, renunciation, divine insight, mathematical intuition, maternal ancestral lineage, surgery, astrology, and release from rebirth.

Chapter 4, Verse 1: Rahu and Ketu as Powerful Yogakarakas. If Rahu or Ketu occupy a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) and are conjoined with or aspected by the lord of a Trikona (5th or 9th), or if they occupy a Trikona and are conjoined with or aspected by the lord of a Kendra, they become powerful Yogakarakas. During their Mahadashas and Antardashas, they confer immense political authority, royal wealth, vehicles, and renown surpassing even natural benefics.

Chapter 4, Verse 2: Retrograde (Vakri) Planets and Their Dynamic Strengths. When a planet is retrograde (Vakri), its strength is equal to that of a planet in its exaltation sign, even if it is positioned in an inimical or friendly sign. However, if a planet is in its exaltation sign and becomes retrograde, it acts as if it were debilitated, producing delayed or humbled fruits. Conversely, if a planet is in its debilitation sign and becomes retrograde, it produces supremely auspicious, exalted-like results, granting unexpected elevation, prosperity, and victory over adversity.

Chapter 5, Verse 1: Principles of Maraka and Longevity in Uttara Kalamrita. The 8th house and the 3rd house are the primary houses of longevity (Ayus). The 12th houses from them—namely the 7th house and the 2nd house—are designated as Maraka houses (houses causing termination of vitality or physical distress). The lords of the 2nd and 7th houses, along with planets placed in them or aspecting them, become Marakas. However, when Maraka dashas operate, fear is unwarranted; the classical remedy is chanting the Maha Mrityunjaya mantra, performing Rudrabhisheka, giving food in charity (Annadāna), and dedicating oneself to righteous conduct, which converts physical fatigue into spiritual purification.
