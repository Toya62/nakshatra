Chapter 1: Rules on Planetary Strengths and Functional Status

Rule 1: If the lord of an angle (Kendra) and the lord of a trine (Trikona) establish mutual reception, conjunction, or aspect, they become Yogakarakas, producing high auspiciousness, professional advancement, and fortune during their respective Vimshottari dasha periods.

Rule 2: Benefic planets (Jupiter, Venus, unafflicted Mercury, Waxing Moon) owning Kendra houses lose their natural beneficence and produce neutral or obstructive effects, whereas natural malefics (Saturn, Mars, Sun) owning Kendra houses shed their maleficence and act constructively.

Rule 3: The 3rd, 6th, and 11th houses are Upachaya houses (houses of growth). Natural malefics placed in these houses promote worldly victory, stamina, competitive success, and progressive wealth as age advances.

Rule 4: The 6th, 8th, and 12th houses are Trik houses (dusthanas). Any bhava lord placed in these houses suffers depletion and obstacles regarding its primary significations, unless involved in a Viparita Raja Yoga.

Rule 5: When the lord of the 6th, 8th, or 12th house is positioned exclusively in another dusthana (6th, 8th, or 12th) without association or aspect of auspicious trine lords, it produces Viparita Raja Yoga (Harsha, Sarala, or Vimala), generating unexpected fortune arising from the adversity of competitors.

Chapter 2: Predictive Rules for Vimshottari Dasha Analysis

Rule 6: The results of a Mahadasha must always be evaluated in conjunction with the Antardasha lord's relationship to the Mahadasha lord. If the Antardasha lord is positioned in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house from the Mahadasha lord (Shadashtaka or Dvidwadasa), that sub-period brings friction, sudden distress, or unfulfilling efforts.

Rule 7: When the Antardasha lord is placed in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) or Trikona (5th, 9th), or in the 11th house from the Mahadasha lord and shares natural or temporal friendship, the period produces exceptional professional elevation, happiness, and material success.

Rule 8: During the Mahadasha of a planet owning the 10th house (Karma Bhava), any Antardasha of a planet occupying the 10th or aspecting the 10th house will activate decisive career milestones, authority promotions, or occupational transformation.

Rule 9: The period of a Maraka lord (lord of the 2nd or 7th house) produces bodily vulnerability, physical strain, or heavy financial expenditures, especially when afflicted by natural malefics or transit Saturn.

Rule 10: If a planet is retrograde (Vakra) in the birth chart, its dasha induces introspection, repeated efforts in its designated significations, and non-linear or unconventional outcomes that manifest suddenly after initial delay.

Chapter 3: Systematic Rules for Transits (Gochara)

Rule 11: Transits (Gochara) must primarily be judged from the natal Moon sign (Chandra Lagna), as the Moon signifies the mind, physical constitution, and immediate experiential reality of the native.

Rule 12: Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st, and 2nd houses from the natal Moon constitutes Sade Sati (a seven-and-a-half-year cycle). The first phase affects expenses and foreign travels, the second phase tests personal health and identity, and the third phase disciplines family finances and speech.

Rule 13: Jupiter transiting the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th, or 11th house from the natal Moon produces benefic expansion, marriage, progeny, wisdom, and financial grace, mitigating several adverse natal dashas.

Rule 14: Transit Rahu or Ketu crossing over natal luminaries (Sun or Moon) or the Lagna axis triggers psychological transformation, karmic reorientation, and sudden shifts in life direction.
