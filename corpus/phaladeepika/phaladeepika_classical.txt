Chapter 6, Verse 1: Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas Definition and Formation. When Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn occupy a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house) identical with their own sign or their exaltation sign, they produce five distinct royal combinations known as the Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas: Ruchaka is formed by Mars; Bhadra is formed by Mercury; Hamsa is formed by Jupiter; Malavya is formed by Venus; and Sasa is formed by Saturn. The person born under these combinations attains eminent stature, noble reputation, leadership, wealth, and virtuous character corresponding to the planet forming the yoga.

Chapter 6, Verse 2: Ruchaka and Bhadra Yoga Specific Results. The person born under Ruchaka Yoga (Mars in Kendra in Aries, Scorpio, or Capricorn) possesses marked physical strength, courage, victorious leadership over adversaries, high administrative or military rank, and steadfast adherence to duty. The person born under Bhadra Yoga (Mercury in Kendra in Gemini or Virgo) possesses eloquence of speech, razor-sharp intellect, deep mastery of shastras and sciences, magnetic physical charm, and enduring distinction in intellectual and state counsels.

Chapter 6, Verse 3: Hamsa and Malavya Yoga Specific Results. The person born under Hamsa Yoga (Jupiter in Kendra in Cancer, Sagittarius, or Pisces) possesses a majestic disposition, pious nature, mastery of sacred philosophy and jurisprudence, profound moral discernment, and universal reverence from rulers and society. The person born under Malavya Yoga (Venus in Kendra in Taurus, Libra, or Pisces) enjoys extraordinary grace, artistic elegance, luxurious conveyances, radiant spouse, mastery of fine arts, and a calm, generous disposition blessed with longevity and domestic felicity.

Chapter 6, Verse 4: Sasa Yoga and Mahapurusha Dignity. The person born under Sasa Yoga (Saturn in Kendra in Libra, Capricorn, or Aquarius) commands authority over villages, institutions, or multitudes. Though serious, reserved, and austere, the native commands steadfast endurance, formidable organizing prowess, political acumen, and sovereign influence gained through persistent perseverance and mastery over labor and resources.

Chapter 6, Verse 5: Gaja Kesari Yoga Formation and Auspicious Fruits. When Jupiter occupies a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house) counted from the Moon, Gaja Kesari Yoga is produced. The native born in this yoga becomes illustrious, endowed with boundless wisdom, overcomes all rivals through dignified conduct, achieves lasting repute, builds lasting charitable institutions, and remains prosperous, virtuous, and respected throughout life.

Chapter 6, Verse 6: Lunar Yogas: Sunapha, Anapha, and Dhurdhura. When planets other than the Sun, Rahu, and Ketu occupy the 2nd house from the Moon, Sunapha Yoga is generated, giving self-earned riches, administrative skill, and keen intellect. When planets occupy the 12th house from the Moon, Anapha Yoga is formed, endowing the native with generous character, physical health, and detachment from worldly anxiety. When planets occupy both the 2nd and 12th houses from the Moon, Dhurdhura Yoga is formed, blessing the person with abundant wealth, trustworthy conveyances, loyal associates, and uninterrupted contentment.

Chapter 6, Verse 7: Kemadruma Yoga and Its Classical Cancellations. When there are no planets in the 2nd and 12th houses from the Moon (excluding Sun and nodes), Kemadruma Yoga is formed, indicating periods of sorrow, isolation, and mental despondency. However, Kemadruma is completely cancelled (Kemadruma Bhanga) if planets occupy Kendras from the Lagna or from the Moon, or if the Moon is conjoined with a benefic planet or aspected by Jupiter. Upon cancellation, the native overcomes early hardships and attains royal fortune.

Chapter 6, Verse 8: Amala Yoga and Auspicious Governance. If a spotless benefic planet (Jupiter, Venus, or Mercury) occupies the 10th house from the Lagna or from the Moon without malefic affliction, Amala Yoga is formed. The native enjoys spotless fame, commands clean professional authority, engages in philanthropic statecraft, remains pure in speech and action, and is honored by sovereigns throughout life.

Chapter 6, Verse 9: Viparita Raja Yogas: Harsha, Sarala, and Vimala. When the lord of the 6th house is placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house, Harsha Yoga is formed, giving invincibility over rivals, radiant health, and unwavering courage. When the lord of the 8th house is placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house, Sarala Yoga is formed, giving longevity, fearlessness, sudden gains, and victory over trials. When the lord of the 12th house is placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house, Vimala Yoga is formed, giving noble character, wise financial accumulation, independence, and spiritual purity. In all three Viparita Raja Yogas, adversity transforms into unexpected elevation provided these lords do not conjoin other Kendra or Trikona lords.

Chapter 6, Verse 10: Neechabhanga Raja Yoga Rules. When a debilitated planet (Neecha) has its dispositor (lord of the debilitation sign) or its exaltation lord in a Kendra from the Lagna or from the Moon, Neechabhanga Raja Yoga is formed. The native, after initial struggle and humbleness, rises to the rank of an emperor or minister, wielding great executive power and enduring wealth.

Chapter 7, Verse 1: Functional Nature of Kendras, Trikonas, and Dusthanas. The 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th houses are Kendras (Vishnusthanas), representing the pillars of action, vitality, domestic happiness, partnerships, and public reputation. The 1st, 5th, and 9th houses are Trikonas (Lakshmisthanas), representing divine grace, intellect, past meritorious karma (Purva Punya), and destiny (Bhagya). The 6th, 8th, and 12th houses are Dusthanas (Trika houses), governing obstacles, debts, chronic ailments, longevity, transformational crises, and expenditure. Benefic planets in Kendras and Trikonas elevate the native, while planets in Upachaya houses (3rd, 6th, 10th, and 11th) flourish through courage and time.

Chapter 19, Verse 1: Principles of Vimshottari Dasha Judgment. In judging the results of a Mahadasha, first examine the natural benefic or malefic disposition of the Dasha lord, its functional house rulership (Bhavesha), its strength in Rashi (D1) and Navamsha (D9), and the houses it aspects. If the Dasha lord is exalted, in its own house, or placed in a Kendra or Trikona with auspicious planets, it produces immense wealth, honors, and joy. If the Dasha lord is debilitated, combust, or placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th house conjoined with cruel malefics, it brings anxiety, physical fatigue, and expenditure.

Chapter 19, Verse 2: Relationship Between Mahadasha and Antardasha Lords. The results of an Antardasha depend heavily upon the mutual relationship between the Mahadasha lord and the Antardasha lord. If the two lords occupy the 1st, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th, or 11th houses mutually from each other, they generate harmonious progress, fulfillment of projects, and family joy. If they occupy the 6th and 8th (Shadashtaka) or 2nd and 12th (Dwidwadasa) positions mutually from each other, the period brings sudden friction, financial fluctuation, loss of harmony, and delays, even if both planets are natural benefics.

Chapter 26, Verse 1: Classical Rules of Planetary Transits (Gochara). Transits of planets must strictly be evaluated from the Janma Rashi (the sign occupied by the Moon at birth) and secondarily from the Lagna. Sun yields auspicious fruits when transiting the 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 11th houses from the Moon. Moon yields auspicious fruits in the 1st, 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th, and 11th houses. Mars produces victory and strength when transiting the 3rd, 6th, and 11th houses. Mercury brings prosperity in the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th, and 11th houses. Jupiter confers supreme divine blessings, honors, and prosperity when transiting the 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th, and 11th houses from the Moon. Venus grants marital bliss and wealth in all houses except the 6th, 7th, and 10th. Saturn yields strength and gains when transiting the 3rd, 6th, and 11th houses.

Chapter 26, Verse 2: Saturn's Sade Sati, Ashtama Shani, and Kantaka Shani. When Saturn transits the 12th, 1st, and 2nd houses from the natal Moon sign, the native undergoes the seven-and-a-half-year cycle termed Sade Sati. This period brings profound psychological restructuring, karmic testing, removal of illusions, and lessons in humility and perseverance. When Saturn transits the 8th house from the natal Moon (Ashtama Shani), the native must guard health, avoid rash financial speculation, and maintain emotional equilibrium. When Saturn transits the 4th house (Kantaka Shani), domestic peace and maternal health require mindful care. These challenging periods are constructive crucibles of dharma, effectively harmonized through Dāna, Japa, Seva, and selfless labor.

Chapter 26, Verse 3: The Principle of Vedha (Obstruction in Transits). A planet transiting a favorable house from the natal Moon sign has its benefic fruits obstructed (Vedha) if another planet (except between Sun and Saturn, or Moon and Mercury) simultaneously transits the corresponding obstruction house. When Vedha occurs, the promised auspicious result is neutralized or delayed until the obstructing planet leaves the Vedha house.

Chapter 10, Verse 1: Kalatra Bhava Definition and Natural Karakas of Marriage. The 7th house (Kalatra Bhava) governs marriage, conjugal happiness, the nature and physical disposition of the spouse, long-term partnerships, and public alliances. Venus (Shukra) is the natural significator (Karaka) of marriage, romance, and aesthetic harmony for all natives, while Jupiter (Guru) acts as the special protector of marital wisdom, progeny, and noble companionship. When the 7th house, its lord, and Venus are well-placed in Kendras or Trikonas and associated with benefics, the native enjoys enduring marital bliss, a virtuous and loyal spouse, and prosperous partnerships.

Chapter 10, Verse 2: Benefics and Malefics in the 7th House. When natural benefics—Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, or full Moon—occupy the 7th house, the spouse is graceful, intelligent, endowed with noble qualities, sweet in speech, and deeply devoted to domestic harmony. When natural malefics occupy the 7th house without benefic aspects, they introduce distinct lessons in temperament: Sun brings a strong-willed, proud spouse requiring ego moderation; Mars imparts passionate energy, quick temper, and dynamic drive (Kuja influence), necessitating conscious patience and emotional maturity; Saturn causes delays in marriage, bringing a mature, serious, and pragmatic spouse who values stability; Rahu brings unconventional or cross-cultural partnerships requiring clear boundaries; and Ketu gives spiritual inclinations or emotional detachment.

Chapter 10, Verse 3: Judgment of Spouse Character from the Navamsha (D9). To ascertain the true temperament, moral character, and spiritual compatibility of the spouse, the wise astrologer must carefully examine the Navamsha of the 7th house (the setting Navamsha) and the disposition of the 7th lord in the Navamsha chart (D9). If the 7th lord or setting Navamsha falls in a sign of Jupiter or Venus, the partner is highly virtuous, dignified, compassionate, and faithful. If it falls in a sign of Mercury, the partner is learned, witty, and clever in business. If it falls in a sign of Mars or Saturn, the partner is austere, bold, intense, or disciplined, requiring mutual compromise and conscious cultivation of emotional harmony.

Chapter 10, Verse 4: Mars and Saturn Influences on the 7th House (Conscious Harmonization). The placement of Mars in the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house (termed Kuja or Manglik influence) does not denote fatal destruction; rather, it indicates an abundance of fiery vitality, assertiveness, and emotional intensity that must be channeled into productive endeavors and shared physical activities. When harmonized through mutual understanding, spiritual maturity, or matching vitality in the partner's chart, this placement provides unwavering loyalty and courageous defense of the family. Similarly, Saturn's aspect or occupation in the 7th delays marriage until personal maturity is attained, cementing an unbreakable, enduring foundation after initial trials.

Chapter 10, Verse 5: Auspicious Yogas for Conjugal Harmony. When the lord of the Lagna and the lord of the 7th house are in mutual friendly signs, in mutual Kendras or Trikonas, or cast benefic aspects upon each other, deep mutual affection, lifelong fidelity, and harmonious cooperation prevail between the spouses. If the 2nd house (family), 4th house (domestic peace), and 7th house (partnership) are free from malefic affliction and guarded by Jupiter or Venus, domestic peace and prosperity flourish uninterrupted.

Chapter 10, Verse 6: Timing of Marriage via Vimshottari Dasha and Gochara Transits. The sacred time of marriage manifests when the native operates the Vimshottari Mahadasha, Antardasha, or Pratyantardasha of: (1) the lord of the 7th house, (2) the lord of the Lagna, (3) natural Karaka Venus, (4) planets placed in or aspecting the 7th house, or (5) the dispositor of the 7th lord in the Navamsha. Simultaneously, astronomical transit (Gochara) triggers must align: Jupiter transiting through or aspecting the 7th house from the Lagna or Moon, or transiting over the natal 7th lord or natal Venus, activates the divine window of matrimonial union and fruitful partnership.

Chapter 11, Verse 1: Karma Bhava Definition and Scope of Professional Destiny. The 10th house (Karma Bhava) is the highest zenith of the sky (Madhya Lagna), governing profession, executive authority, public reputation, worldly status, leadership, and righteous vocation. The 10th lord, its placement, and the planets occupying the 10th house determine how the native manifests their divine purpose (Swadharma) and attains honor in society. An unblemished 10th house blesses the native with clean authority, social respect, and enduring legacy.

Chapter 11, Verse 2: Sun and Mars in the 10th House (Digbala and Executive Prowess). Sun and Mars achieve highest directional strength (Digbala) in the 10th house. A native with Sun in the 10th achieves radiant leadership, governmental patronage, executive prestige, and unquestioned public authority. Mars in the 10th creates a dynamic, pioneering commander, engineer, surgeon, or industrial leader who triumphs over complex obstacles and executes ambitious projects through tireless fortitude.

Chapter 11, Verse 3: Benefics in the 10th House (Intellectual and Ethical Careers). Jupiter in the 10th house produces esteemed judges, ministers, preceptors, financial authorities, and noble philosophers who guide institutions with moral integrity. Mercury in the 10th confers renown in commerce, communications, literature, analysis, and diplomacy. Venus in the 10th elevates artists, designers, diplomats, and hospitality leaders, bringing wealth through creative arts and gracious public relations.

Chapter 11, Verse 4: Saturn and Rahu in the 10th House (Mass Leadership and Resilience). Saturn in the 10th house raises the native through steady, dedicated toil, making them an indispensable organizer, industrial manager, or leader of the working populace. Though early career requires patience and discipline, Saturn bestows immense, enduring authority in the second half of life. Rahu in the 10th house gives extraordinary ambition, unconventional breakthroughs, foreign assignments, and rapid ascent in modern technological or political arenas.

Chapter 11, Verse 5: Timing of Professional Advancement and Career Milestones. Professional elevation, promotions, and auspicious career transitions materialize during the operational periods (Mahadasha, Antardasha, or Pratyantardasha) of: (1) the 10th house lord, (2) planets occupying the 10th house, (3) the 6th lord (service, daily employment, and triumph over rivals), (4) the 11th lord (gains and promotions), or (5) the 2nd lord (accumulated wealth). When Jupiter or Saturn in Gochara transit casts an auspicious aspect on the 10th house or natal 10th lord, substantial professional recognition and occupational consolidation take place.
