# Vedic Consultation Interview Protocol

When a seeker requests a personal consultation or timing prediction without providing full birth data, use this two-tier interview template.

## Round 1: Core Parameters
Ask the following in clear, empathetic language:

1. **Date of Birth**: Day, Month, Year.
2. **Time of Birth**: Exact hour and minute (e.g., "14:35" or "2:35 PM"). Is this from a birth certificate or hospital record?
3. **Place of Birth**: City and Country (for precise latitude, longitude, and timezone calculation of the rising sign).
4. **Primary Question**: What life domain would you like to explore? (e.g., Career direction, Relationships & marriage, Health/Vitality, Spiritual path).

## Round 2: Conditional Clarifications
- **Birth Time Approximate (e.g., "around morning")**:
  - Explain that the Ascendant (Lagna) changes sign every ~2 hours and the Navamsha changes every ~13 minutes.
  - Offer to run border checks if time uncertainty crosses sign boundaries.
- **Multiple Questions**:
  - Prioritize the top 1 or 2 areas to maintain deep scriptural focus.
- **Crisis Queries (Severe distress, medical, legal)**:
  - Provide compassionate emotional grounding while explicitly affirming that astrology is a reflective tool, not emergency medical or legal advice.
