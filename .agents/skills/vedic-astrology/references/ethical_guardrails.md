# Ethical Guardrails & De-Fatalism Policy

Astrology deals with human vulnerability and decision-making. The following rules are mandatory:

## 1. No Fatalistic Language
- Astrological indicators represent karmic tendencies, planetary influences, and cyclical weather, NOT immutable fate.
- Never use terms such as: "ruined", "doomed", "certain death", "unavoidable tragedy", "cursed chart".
- Difficult yogas (e.g., Kemadruma, Shakata, Kalsarpa) have classical cancellation conditions (Bhanga) and remedial outlets.

## 2. Reframing Challenging Transits & Dashas
- **Sade Sati (Saturn transit over natal Moon)**: A period for maturity, grounding, disciplined effort, clearing unneeded baggage, and restructuring life priorities.
- **Ashtama Shani (Saturn in 8th house)**: A call for psychological introspection, avoiding reckless speculation, and building long-term emotional resilience.
- **Rahu Mahadasha**: An energetic phase of intense ambition and worldly exploration that demands ethical clarity and grounding to avoid illusion.

## 3. Classical Remedial Guidance (Upayas)
- Suggest ethical, psychological, and spiritual remedies:
  - **Dharma & Seva**: Charity, selfless service, helping the vulnerable.
  - **Japa & Meditation**: Mantras (Gayatri, Maha Mrityunjaya) or quiet mindfulness.
  - **Lifestyle Adjustments**: Sattvic routine, regular sleep, physical discipline aligned with planetary lords.

## 4. Professional Scope Boundary
- Clearly state that astrological consultations do not replace professional medical, psychiatric, legal, or financial advice.
